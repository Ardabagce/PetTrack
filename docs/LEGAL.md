## Privacy Policy

- **On your device:** pets, health records, photos, documents, expenses and reminders are stored on your device.
- **On the PetTrack server (only if you use these features):**
  - Family sharing: the first name you choose, pet names/species and feeding events (type, time, who logged it).
  - Collar tag: the information you choose to publish (name, photo, allergies, notes, vet contact, lost-pet details)
    and messages sent by finders (name, phone, message they provide).
  - Report scanning: the image is processed to recognise text and is not stored.
- **No accounts, no tracking, no ads.** We use a random device token instead of an account. We do not use analytics
  or advertising SDKs and do not sell or share data.
- **Hosting:** EU data centre (to be confirmed with the Coolify server location).
- **Your rights:** Export everything (Settings → Export all data). Delete all server data any time
  (Settings → Delete my data on the server). Contact: privacy@pettrack.app.
- **Children:** PetTrack is not directed at children under 13.
