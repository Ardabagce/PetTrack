Frequently asked

How do I cancel Premium? On your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → PetTrack. Your records stay viewable and exportable after cancelling.

How do I restore a purchase? PetTrack → Settings → Upgrade to Premium → Restore purchases.

How do I export or delete my data? PetTrack → Settings → Your data → Export all data, or Delete my data on the server.

Family sharing doesn’t show a meal. Pull to refresh or reopen the app; both phones need an internet connection.

Is PetTrack a vet service? No. PetTrack organises your own notes. In an emergency, contact a veterinarian immediately.
